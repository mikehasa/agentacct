## Scenario

015 — Dark appearance with saturated threshold colors.

## Verdict

Accept with visual restraint.

## Findings

The planned dark amber and coral are legible semantic colors, and the existing meter also carries percentage text and 75/90 notches. In a full-width ledger, however, several saturated fills could dominate the quieter consumption facts and make ordinary attention look like failure.

## Recommendation

Keep threshold color inside the meter and compact status text only. Do not tint whole rows; preserve neutral dividers and ink labels. Reserve coral strictly for 100% or more.

## Test idea

Snapshot dark mode with rows at 74%, 75%, 90%, and 100%; verify hierarchy, text contrast, and that coral appears only on the exhausted row.
